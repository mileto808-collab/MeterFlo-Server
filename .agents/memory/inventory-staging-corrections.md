---
name: Staged stock correction policy
description: User-approved correction of recorded picks and physical staging mistakes before vehicle handoff.
---

Technicians must be able to report a pickup problem, and authorized warehouse users must be able to correct staged meters, serials, parts and quantities before handoff without releasing and rebuilding the batch.

**Why:** The user described both technician-discovered and warehouse-discovered mistakes after a committed staging pull. An easy correction process must preserve the original evidence and avoid pulling the same stock twice.

**How to apply:** Distinguish recording corrections, actual physical returns/exchanges, and missing stock. Verify the resulting stock actually waiting at staging. Preserve delivered custody and original receipts. Pause pickup for reported problems until reviewed; update the crew's in-app notice and revisioned pick ticket after resolution.

Missing stock must never be treated as a verified return to the source warehouse.

**Why:** An unknown physical location is not proof of available inventory elsewhere. Crediting it automatically would create usable stock that may not exist.

**How to apply:** Remove confirmed missing units from usable staging stock and retain a visible investigation. A confirmed recovery restores only the exact missing units to a verified location; a write-off adds nothing.

An actual exchange can have no net book-stock change when the initial staging entry already named the desired item but a different physical item was pulled.

**Why:** Recording that exchange as another full pull would double-deduct correct stock or double-credit the unexpected item.

**How to apply:** Keep explicit physical-return evidence separate from net ledger corrections. Verify unexpected items are accounted at the source, rather than crediting them again.
