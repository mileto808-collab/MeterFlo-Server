---
name: Defective replacement policy
description: Physical follow-up visits versus accounting retries and original customer completion delivery.
---

A post-completion defective replacement is another physical visit, not a reset of the original installation. Preserve original consumption; returned defective equipment is pending receiving/quarantine, never an automatic stock refund.

**Why:** The approved replacement workflow must preserve the installation chain and distinguish two real units used from a retry of one recorded installation.

**How to apply:** Baseline each equipment kind from its latest completed installation, including chains of partial replacements. If prior accounting is delayed, accept valid field evidence but reconcile the chain in physical order; never create another replacement to fix bookkeeping.

Do not send a follow-up through the original customer completion contract without an explicitly supported replacement-visit shape.

**Why:** A linked visit is not a second completion of the utility's original order. Reusing that contract could overwrite the customer's installed-device record or duplicate billing.

**How to apply:** Keep replacement evidence/history locally and introduce customer delivery only after agreeing the replacement contract; absence of delivery must remain explicit.

Do not create the mobile-agent handoff until the replacement workflow is working and verified.

**Why:** The user asked to get this working before creating the handoff.

**How to apply:** Finish and verify the warehouse-fulfilled and onboard replacement paths before preparing or updating the mobile handoff.
